Cookie Policy

Effective date: {% placeholder "effective_date" %}

We only use cookies the site can’t work without. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies. That’s why there’s no “accept all / reject all” choice: there is nothing optional to switch on.

Cookies we set

NamePurposeLifetimeType
sessionidKeeps you signed in, and holds the temporary security token used while you sign in with osu!.Up to 30 days, or until you sign outStrictly necessary
csrftokenProtects forms and game requests from cross-site forgery.Up to 1 yearStrictly necessary

Both are first-party, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and sent over HTTPS only. Strictly necessary cookies don’t need your consent under the EU ePrivacy rules, but we tell you about them here and in the banner.

Other browser storage

When you dismiss the cookie notice we remember that in your browser’s local storage (osu_trivia_cookie_notice), so we don’t show it again. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Third parties

Your avatar is loaded from osu!’s servers (a.ppy.sh). That request goes from your browser to them, and osu! may set or read its own cookies under its own policy. We don’t embed any other third-party scripts, fonts or trackers; fonts are served from this site.

Body text uses Exo 2 (SIL Open Font License). Flag glyphs use Twemoji Country Flags; graphics © Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, licensed CC-BY 4.0. Both are self-hosted on this site.

If this changes

If we ever add anything non-essential, such as analytics, it will be off by default and will only turn on if you opt in through a clear choice on this site. You can also clear or block cookies in your browser at any time; you’ll just be signed out and need to sign in again to play.
